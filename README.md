## 🩺 The Idea

**Jeevanta** is a healthcare analytics solution designed to bring hospital information into one connected analytical experience.

The project transforms healthcare data into meaningful insights across:

**Patients · Clinical Activity · Finance · Doctors · Resources · Pharmacy**

Rather than treating each area as an isolated report, Jeevanta creates a unified view of hospital performance and patient-related information through interactive Power BI dashboards.

> **The goal is simple: make complex healthcare data easier to understand, explore and act upon.**

# 📊 The Jeevanta Experience

## 01 — 🏥 Executive Overview

The primary management view of the hospital.

Provides a high-level understanding of hospital performance through key operational, patient and financial indicators.

### Focus

`Hospital KPIs` · `Patients` · `Admissions` · `Discharges` · `Performance`

---

## 02 — 👤 Patient & Clinical Insights

Focuses on the patient population and clinical activity within the hospital.

### Focus

`Patient Demographics` · `Clinical Insights` · `Admissions` · `Disease Analysis` · `Patient Metrics`

---

## 03 — 💰 Financial & Billing Insights

A dedicated analytical view of the hospital's financial and billing ecosystem.

### Focus

`Billing` · `Charges` · `Payments` · `Insurance` · `Financial Trends`

---

## 04 — 👨‍⚕️ Doctors & Resource Insights

Explores the people, departments and infrastructure involved in hospital operations.

### Focus

`Doctors` · `Departments` · `Wards` · `Beds` · `Resource Utilization`

---

## 05 — 💊 Pharmacy & Resource Intelligence

A dedicated view into medicine utilization and pharmacy resources.

### Focus

`Prescription Trends` · `Medicine Cost` · `Quantity Dispensed` · `Stock Status` · `Expiry Alerts`

---

# 06 — 🔎 Patient 360°

### A closer look at the individual patient.

Patient 360° is the dedicated **drill-through page** of Jeevanta.

Selecting a patient from the relevant dashboard interaction takes the user into a focused patient-level view.

### Current Patient 360° Experience

**Patient Profile**

Provides key demographic information such as:

- Patient ID
- Age
- Gender
- Blood Group
- Date of Birth
- City

**Patient Journey**

Provides a visual overview of the patient's admission journey, including relevant admission and discharge context.

**Admission Details**

Brings together important admission information such as:

- Admission ID
- Admission Date
- Admission Type
- Admission Status
- Department
- Ward
- Bed
- Disease ID
- Discharge Date
- Discharge Status

> **Patient 360° shifts the dashboard from “What is happening?” to “What is happening with this patient?”**
