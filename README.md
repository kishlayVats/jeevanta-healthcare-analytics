> 🩺 **Turning Healthcare Data into Meaningful Patient & Hospital Insights**

**Jeevanta** is an end-to-end **Healthcare Analytics & Business Intelligence solution** built in Power BI to transform hospital data into an interactive, decision-focused analytical experience.

The dashboard brings together **patients, clinical activity, hospital operations, doctors, resources, pharmacy, billing, insurance and patient journeys** into one unified healthcare analytics platform.

# 🩺 What is Jeevanta?

**Jeevanta** is designed as a centralized healthcare analytics platform that helps transform hospital data into actionable insights.

Instead of viewing patient, clinical, financial and operational information separately, Jeevanta connects these areas to provide a more complete view of hospital performance.
